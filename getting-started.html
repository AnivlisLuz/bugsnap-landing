Get started in 5 minutes

Install BugSnap, connect your bug tracker, and send your first report — step by step.

Phase 1 of 3
Install the extension
BugSnap is in beta — installation is manual via Chrome Developer Mode.
1
Download the extension
Download bugsnap-extension-demo.zip from this page.
2
Unzip the file
Double-click the zip to extract it. You'll get a folder called bugsnap-extension — keep it somewhere permanent (don't delete it).
3
Open Chrome extensions
In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions in the address bar.
4
Enable Developer Mode
Toggle Developer mode on — it's in the top-right corner of the extensions page.
5
Load the extension
Click "Load unpacked" → select the bugsnap-extension folder you unzipped. The 🐛 icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar.
💡 If you don't see the icon, click the puzzle piece (🧩) in Chrome's toolbar and pin BugSnap to keep it visible.
Phase 2 of 3
Connect your bug tracker
Choose where your bug reports will be sent — Azure DevOps, Jira, or GitHub.
1
Open BugSnap and sign in
Click the 🐛 icon in your toolbar. Sign in with your Google account when prompted.
2
Open Settings
Click the ⚙️ gear icon in the top-right of the popup to open the Settings page.
3
Connect your platform
Choose one of the three integrations and follow the OAuth flow:
☁️
Azure DevOps
Enter your organization name and select a project
🔷
Jira
Sign in to Atlassian and select your site and project
🐙
GitHub
Authorize BugSnap and select a repository
⚠️ Beta note: OAuth popups may ask you to authorize a few permissions. This is normal — BugSnap only requests access to create issues.
Phase 3 of 3
Send your first bug report
Navigate to any web app and report a bug in under 60 seconds.
1
Navigate to the bug
Go to the page where the bug happens. The extension captures the current state automatically when you open it.
2
Click the BugSnap icon
The popup opens and instantly captures a screenshot, the URL, browser info, OS, screen resolution, and any console errors.
3
Annotate (optional)
Click "✏️ Mark area on screenshot" to highlight the bug with a rectangle, arrow, or freehand drawing.
4
Fill in the title
Type a short bug title and select severity. Optionally add a description.

Or click "✨ Auto-fill with AI" to let Claude analyze the screenshot and fill everything automatically (3 free uses/month).
5
Send the report
Click your connected platform button — Azure DevOps, Jira, or GitHub. The issue is created with the full report, screenshot, and console logs attached.
✅ Done! You'll see a green success message with a link to the created issue. Click it to verify everything arrived correctly.
